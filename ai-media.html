What I want to study

Consent, data licensing and accountability in AI-mediated media

How media and commerce platforms obtain, license and use people's data for AI systems, what meaningful consent looks like in practice, and who is accountable when these systems fail, with a focus on emerging markets like Nigeria.

  1. Consent. How do users in emerging markets understand and give consent when platforms use their data to personalise content or train AI, and where does that consent break down?
  2. Licensing and accountability. What data licensing and consent frameworks do newsrooms and platforms rely on for AI systems, and who is held accountable when those systems produce errors or harm?
  3. Translation. How do AI governance and data privacy frameworks written elsewhere translate, or fail to translate, into media and business practice in countries like Nigeria?
Methods

Interviews and ethnography with practitioners, document and policy analysis, and surveys of users.
